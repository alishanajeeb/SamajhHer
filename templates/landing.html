FAQs
Aksar pooche jaane wale sawal
Kya yeh platform free hai? +
Haan — SamajhHer bilkul free hai. Koi hidden charges nahi, koi subscription nahi. Hamesha free rahega.
Kya meri baat private rahegi? +
Bilkul. Aapki conversations sirf aapke account mein save hoti hain. Koi aur nahi dekh sakta — na family, na doctor, na hum.
Kya yeh doctor ki jagah le sakta hai? +
Nahi — SamajhHer doctor ki jagah nahi leta. Yeh aapko samjhata hai ke kya ho raha hai aur doctor ke paas jaane ki tayari karta hai. Doctor se milna hamesha zaroori hai.
Kya family bhi use kar sakti hai? +
Haan! "Ghar Walon Ko Samjhao" flow specifically husband, bete, betiyon ke liye bana hai taake woh samjhein ke ammi ya biwi ke saath kya ho raha hai aur kaise madad karein.
Voice feature kaise kaam karta hai? +
Mic button press karein aur Urdu ya English mein bolein — AI sun kar samjhega. Chrome browser mein best kaam karta hai.
